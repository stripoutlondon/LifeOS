Life OS support

Life OS is currently in private beta. If something does not work, please include the device you are using and a short description of what happened.

Quick help

  • Install on iPhone: open Life OS in Safari, tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, then confirm Add.
  • Voice input: tap Speak beside a text field and allow microphone access. Apple keyboard dictation remains available from the keyboard microphone.
  • Cloud sign-in: open Profile, choose Connect cloud, then follow the secure link sent to your email.
  • Family sharing: one adult creates the family space and copies its invitation code. The other adult signs into their own account and joins with that code.

Report an issue or request deletion

Open a support request through the Life OS project. Do not include journal entries, health information or passwords in a public report.
