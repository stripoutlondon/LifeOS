YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

Life OS is designed to keep personal reflections personal. You can use the app locally without creating an account. If you connect cloud backup, the app stores the information needed to synchronise your profile across your devices.

Information you choose to add

  • Your name, family name, purpose and principles.
  • Priorities, routines, goals, journal entries and check-ins.
  • Health and habit information you choose to record, such as alcohol-free days and cigarette counts.
  • Your email address when you request a secure sign-in link.

How it is used

Your information is used to operate Life OS, save your progress and synchronise it across your devices. We do not sell personal information or use private journal, health or goal data for advertising.

Family spaces

Family spaces share only the family-space name and family principles with invited members. Each person’s priorities, goals, journal, health data and daily check-ins remain private to their own account.

Storage and security

Without cloud connection, data remains in your browser’s local storage. With cloud connection, data is stored by Supabase and protected by account-level database access rules. Email sign-in links are handled by Supabase authentication.

Your choices

You can export or delete the active local profile from the Profile screen. You may also sign out of cloud backup at any time. For account-data deletion during the beta, use the support page.

Microphone

Voice input uses browser or device speech-recognition features when available. Life OS uses the resulting text in the field you selected; it does not intentionally store an audio recording.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the beta gains features such as Apple sign-in, subscriptions or wearable integrations. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
